# Expression

Notable should feel friendly, warm, and a little playful. Its character is approachable and human. It is not sterile, industrial, or corporate. Expression is carried by shape, motion, typography, and the way feedback responds to the user.

## Warmth through shape

Rounded forms and soft curves make interactive elements feel approachable. Sharp corners and hard edges read as cold, so they are avoided for interactive surfaces. Shape is one of the first things a person notices, and it sets the tone for everything else.

## Personality through motion

Motion is part of Notable's identity. Movement should feel fluid and responsive, as if objects have weight and follow the user's attention. A well-timed transition can say more about the product than any color.

## Playful, not childish

Playful choices stay within a restrained, adult range. Exaggerated bounce, cartoonish treatments, and decoration that competes with content weaken the product. A small amount of surprise in a moment of feedback is welcome. Constant surprise is not.

## Voice through type

Typography carries part of this character, but readability comes first. A warm voice that reduces legibility works against expression. See [Typography](../foundations/typography.md).

## Principle

Expression should make Notable feel like a considered product made for people. It must never come at the cost of clarity or access.
