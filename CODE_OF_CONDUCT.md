# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

Notable is built around the idea that thoughtful software can be expressive, accessible, and welcoming. We want the Notable community to be a friendly place for everyone who participates in the project, regardless of their background, identity, experience level, or point of view.

As contributors and maintainers, we pledge to make participation in the Notable community a respectful experience for everyone, free from harassment, intimidation, and discrimination.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful, considerate, and constructive.
- Welcoming newcomers and helping them learn.
- Giving and receiving feedback gracefully.
- Discussing ideas and technical decisions openly while treating people with respect.
- Focusing criticism on code, proposals, and decisions rather than attacking people.
- Respecting different perspectives, experiences, and communication styles.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
- Helping keep discussions focused, useful, and collaborative.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory, hateful, or demeaning language or conduct.
- Sexualized language or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or repeated bad-faith arguments.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Insulting or humiliating others, including through hostile code reviews or issue discussions.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional, collaborative community.

Disagreement is a normal part of building software. It is not an excuse for disrespectful behavior.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all spaces where the Notable community interacts, including:

- GitHub repositories, issues, pull requests, and discussions.
- Code reviews and project-related communication.
- Community spaces or events when someone is representing Notable.
- Other spaces where conduct related to Notable has a meaningful impact on community members.

It applies equally to maintainers, contributors, and other participants.

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards in this Code of Conduct. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate it, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when appropriate.

Maintainers should explain moderation decisions when practical, handle reports with care, and apply these standards consistently.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, report it privately to the Notable maintainers.

You can contact the maintainers through the contact method listed in the repository profile or project documentation. Please include, if possible:

- A description of what happened.
- Links to relevant issues, pull requests, comments, or other material.
- Any context that may help the maintainers understand the situation.

Please avoid making sensitive reports public when a private reporting route is available. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers will review each report and determine an appropriate response based on the circumstances.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take any action they consider appropriate to stop unacceptable behavior and protect the community. Responses may include:

1. A private or public warning.
2. Removal or editing of inappropriate content.
3. A temporary restriction from participating in project spaces.
4. A permanent ban from project spaces.

The response will depend on the severity and context of the behavior. Repeated violations may result in stronger action.

Anyone asked to stop unacceptable behavior is expected to comply immediately. Maintainers may remove participation privileges without a prior warning when the situation warrants it.

## Fairness and Good-Faith Participation

Reports will be reviewed in good faith. Retaliation against someone for reporting a concern, helping with a report, or participating in an investigation is not acceptable.

This Code of Conduct is intended to protect people and support productive collaboration, not to prevent respectful disagreement or good-faith technical criticism.
